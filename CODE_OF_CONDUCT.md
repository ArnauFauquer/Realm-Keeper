# Code of Conduct

Realm Keeper is a small project built around a shared hobby. We want everyone
who takes part — in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the
project lives — to feel welcome, whatever their background, identity or level
of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Give and receive feedback about the work, not the person.
- Respect differing opinions, play styles and ways of running a table.
- Own your mistakes and apologize to those affected.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Discriminatory jokes or remarks about any group.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or trolling.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  friendly, public space.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, and when someone represents the
project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer
privately through their GitHub profile,
[@ArnauFauquer](https://github.com/ArnauFauquer). Reports are handled
confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may, at their discretion, respond to a violation by:

1. **Correction** — a private note explaining what was inappropriate.
2. **Warning** — a warning with consequences for continued behavior.
3. **Temporary ban** — a time-limited ban from interacting with the project.
4. **Permanent ban** — for repeated or severe violations.

Comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code may be
edited or removed.
