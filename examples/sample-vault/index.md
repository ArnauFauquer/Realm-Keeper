# Welcome to Realm Keeper

This is a sample vault: a folder of Markdown notes, the same as an Obsidian
vault. Point the app at yours with `VAULT_DIR` and these notes go away.

## Try it

- Follow a link: [[The Rusty Anchor]], or meet [[Captain Mirela|the captain]].
- Roll some dice: `2d6+1`, a check with advantage `adv+4`, or a Hope & Fear roll `hf+2`.
- Press the die in the table below to roll on it.

| d6  | Weather at sea      |
| --- | ------------------- |
| 1-2 | Fog rolls in        |
| 3-4 | A steady wind       |
| 5   | Squall on the bow   |
| 6   | Dead calm           |

## Then

- Edit a note with the pencil button: it is written straight back to its file.
- Open the **Observatory** to make maps (charts), scenes (vistas), characters,
  encounters and battlemaps.
- Upload music to the **player** and send things to a second screen for your players.
