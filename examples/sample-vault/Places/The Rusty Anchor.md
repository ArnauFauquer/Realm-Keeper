# The Rusty Anchor

A dockside tavern that smells of tar and fried fish. [[People/Captain Mirela]]
keeps a corner table and a running tab nobody dares to collect.

**Rumours** (roll `1d4`):

1. A ghost ship was seen past the breakwater.
2. The harbourmaster is selling berths twice.
3. Someone is paying in coins nobody can place.
4. Mirela is hiring, and not asking questions.
