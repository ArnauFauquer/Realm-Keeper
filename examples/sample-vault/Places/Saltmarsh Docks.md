# Saltmarsh Docks

Six crooked piers, a fish market and too many lanterns. [[Harbourmaster Quill]]
counts every crate that comes ashore, and some that don't.

- Drinks at [[The Rusty Anchor]].
- The *Gull's Debt* is moored at the last pier: see [[The Gull's Debt]].
- On foggy nights, people swear they hear [[The Ghost Ship|a bell out at sea]].

**Something on the pier** (roll `1d6`):

| d6 | You find               |
| -- | ---------------------- |
| 1  | A crate of live eels   |
| 2  | A ledger, half burnt   |
| 3  | A drunk sailor, asleep |
| 4  | Quill's spy, pretending to fish |
| 5  | A wet map of the reef  |
| 6  | A bell, still ringing  |
