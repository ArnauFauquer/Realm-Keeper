# Harbourmaster Quill

Thin, polite, and always writing. Quill sells each berth at
[[Saltmarsh Docks]] twice and keeps both payments.

- **Wants:** [[Captain Mirela]]'s ship, and her silence.
- **Knows:** what the [[The Ghost Ship|ghost ship]] really carries.
- **Insight to catch the lie:** `1d20+3`
