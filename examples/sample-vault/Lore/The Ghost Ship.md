# The Ghost Ship

A black hull with no lights, seen past the breakwater on foggy nights. The
sailors at [[The Rusty Anchor]] say it rings a bell before it vanishes.

> [!secret] For the GM
> It is no ghost: it is [[Harbourmaster Quill]]'s smuggling barge,
> painted black, and the bell warns the boats waiting at the reef.

**Fear check when it appears:** `hf+1`
